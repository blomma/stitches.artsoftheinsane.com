How it works

Start even, then make it fit.

Stitches spaces everything evenly to begin with. If that doesn’t suit your stitch pattern, move things until it does.

01 / Set it up

Tell it what you have and what you need.

Enter your stitch count and how many stitches to increase or decrease. Say whether you’re knitting flat or in the round, pick the increase or decrease you’re using, and for a flat row, how much to leave at each edge. Stitches spaces them evenly, draws where each one falls and tells you how many you’ll have at the end.

02 / Shape it

Put the shaping where you want it.

Even isn’t always right. A decrease might land in the middle of a cable, or you might want it lined up with the lace below. Touch and hold a decrease to slide it along, or tap it and step it a stitch at a time; the gaps on either side give and take. Lock the ones that have to stay put, and rebalance to space the rest evenly around them. Orange shows where one side no longer mirrors the other. The written row changes to match, and you can undo anything.

03 / Knit it

Written out the way a pattern would.

You get a row like “Decrease row (RS): K5, k2tog, [k10, k2tog] 4 times, k5 (55 sts).” Choose brackets, parentheses or asterisks for the repeats. If a whole row is too much to keep in your head, open it step by step. Share it to Notes, Messages or wherever you keep your project notes.

04 / Keep it

Pick up the plan when you pick up your knitting.

Save a plan under your project’s name. Tap it later and it opens straight in the planner, where every change you make is kept. With iCloud, your saved plans are on both your iPhone and iPad, ready when you reach that part of the pattern.

The details

The fiddly bits, handled.

Enough choice to match your pattern, without turning one row into a spreadsheet.

  • 01

    The stitches you use

    Decrease with k2tog, ssk or p2tog. Increase with M1L, M1R, kfb, yo, M1P or pfb. Stitches knows a kfb uses up a stitch and an M1 doesn’t, so the counts come out right. Pick a purl stitch and the rest of the row is purled too.

  • 02

    Edges and markers

    On a flat row, leave half a gap at each end to centre the shaping, or keep every gap the same when the edge will show, like an open front or a picked-up band. In the round, the shaping is mirrored around your marker whenever the numbers allow.

  • 03

    Fingers, buttons or VoiceOver

    Drag a handle on the diagram, or tap it and nudge it with the arrow buttons. Tap a gap to see its count. VoiceOver reads out every gap and handle, says when the spacing is out of balance, and can move or lock any increase or decrease. The text follows your device’s text size.

  • 04

    A small, quiet app

    It works offline. There’s no account to make, no ads and no subscription.

Your work stays yours

No one’s watching.

Stitches only knows the numbers and names you type in. There’s no analytics, no tracking and no account.

On your devices

The calculator works offline. Saved plans live in the app on your iPhone or iPad, and you can change or delete them whenever you like.

Across your devices

If you use iCloud, Apple syncs your saved plans through your own private iCloud storage. There’s no Stitches server, and the developer never sees your plans.

Privacy Policy — Stitches

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version: The developer does not collect your personal data through Stitches. Your plans are stored on your device and, when iCloud sync is available, in your private iCloud database. There is no app account, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information you enter

Stitches uses the stitch counts, shaping choices and plan names you enter to calculate placement and write knitting instructions. Saved plans remain in the app's storage on your device.

iCloud sync

Stitches uses Apple's SwiftData and CloudKit services to sync saved plans between devices signed in to your Apple Account. Those records are stored in your private iCloud database under Apple's control and your iCloud settings. The developer does not operate a server for Stitches or receive your plans. The calculator can be used offline; sync resumes when Apple can connect your devices to iCloud.

What the developer collects

In short, nothing. Stitches has no analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, crash reporting service or third-party SDKs. The developer cannot see the contents of your plans or how you use the app.

Your control

You can edit and delete saved plans in Stitches. You can manage Stitches' iCloud data and iCloud access in your device's Apple Account settings. Deleting the app removes its local data, but iCloud records or device backups may remain under your Apple settings until you remove them there.

This website
