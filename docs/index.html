From numbers to needles

A plan you can see and use.

Stitches gives you a balanced starting point, then lets you adjust it to the knitting in front of you.

01 / Set it up

Tell it what you have and what you need.

Enter your starting stitches and the number to increase or decrease. Choose a flat row or the round, your shaping stitch, and how the edges should be spaced. Stitches works out the gaps and the stitch count after the row.

02 / Shape it

Move a shaping point. The rest falls into place.

See the spacing on a diagram. Press and drag a gap or shaping point to put it where your knitting needs it; the remaining gaps adjust around the change. Undo is there if you want to try another arrangement.

03 / Knit it

From a diagram to a line of pattern.

Stitches turns your plan into standard knitting notation. Choose brackets, parentheses or asterisks for repeats. Read it step by step when you need a little more room, or share the instruction with your project notes.

04 / Keep it

Pick up the plan when you pick up your knitting.

Save plans under your project names and edit them later. Your saved plans sync through your private iCloud account between iPhone and iPad, so the shaping is there when you reach that part of the pattern.

The small things matter

Made for the way knitters work.

Enough control to match your project, without turning a simple calculation into a spreadsheet.

  • 01

    Stitches you actually use

    Decrease with k2tog, ssk or p2tog. Increase with M1L, M1R, kfb, yo, M1P or pfb. Stitches accounts for the difference between working into a stitch and making one between stitches.

  • 02

    Edges that make sense

    Use narrower half-width gaps at the ends of a row to centre the shaping, or equal gaps throughout. For a round, move the beginning-of-round marker to suit your work.

  • 03

    Room for your hands

    The diagram supports dragging and button controls. VoiceOver can describe and move each gap, and the text scales with your device settings.

  • 04

    A quiet tool

    Work offline, save a plan, and return to it later. There is no app account, no advertising and no subscription.

Your work stays yours

Private by design.

Stitches works with the numbers and names you enter. It has no analytics or tracking and no account to create.

On your devices

The calculator works offline. Saved plans live in the app on your iPhone or iPad. Your stitch counts and project names are yours to edit or remove.

Across your devices

If iCloud is available, Apple syncs saved plans through your private iCloud database. Stitches has no developer-operated server and does not receive a copy of your plans.

Privacy Policy — Stitches

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version: The developer does not collect your personal data through Stitches. Your plans are stored on your device and, when iCloud sync is available, in your private iCloud database. There is no app account, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information you enter

Stitches uses the stitch counts, shaping choices and plan names you enter to calculate placement and write knitting instructions. Saved plans remain in the app's storage on your device.

iCloud sync

Stitches uses Apple's SwiftData and CloudKit services to sync saved plans between devices signed in to your Apple Account. Those records are stored in your private iCloud database under Apple's control and your iCloud settings. The developer does not operate a server for Stitches or receive your plans. The calculator can be used offline; sync resumes when Apple can connect your devices to iCloud.

What the developer collects

In short, nothing. Stitches has no analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, crash reporting service or third-party SDKs. The developer cannot see the contents of your plans or how you use the app.

Your control

You can edit and delete saved plans in Stitches. You can manage Stitches' iCloud data and iCloud access in your device's Apple Account settings. Deleting the app removes its local data, but iCloud records or device backups may remain under your Apple settings until you remove them there.
