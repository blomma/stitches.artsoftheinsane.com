How it works

Planning a row

Stitches spaces everything evenly to start with. If that doesn’t suit your stitch pattern, you can move any of it.

01 / Setup

Enter your numbers

Enter your stitch count, how many stitches to increase or decrease, and the increase or decrease you’re using. You can knit flat or in the round, and on a flat row you also choose how much to leave at each edge. You’ll see the count you end up with and a diagram of where each one falls.

02 / Adjusting

Move the shaping

Even spacing sometimes puts a decrease in the middle of a cable. Touch and hold it to slide it along (on a Mac, drag it), or tap it and use the arrow buttons to move it one stitch at a time. The gaps on either side and the written row change with it, and any move can be undone. If some of the decreases have to stay where they are, lock them, and Rebalance Spacing spaces the rest evenly between them.

Spacing that doesn’t match the opposite side is orange. Tap a gap to see how many stitches are in it. The numbers under the diagram are the gap sizes in the row, and tapping the 7, say, puts a coloured dot on every gap of 7 stitches.

03 / The written row

Written out like a pattern

Five decreases over 60 stitches come out as “Decrease row (RS): K5, k2tog, [k10, k2tog] 4 times, k5 (55 sts).” If you’re used to parentheses or asterisks for repeats, you can switch to those. Step by Step shows a long row as a list of single steps. To keep the row with your project notes, share it to Notes or another app.

04 / Saved plans

Save plans for later

Save a plan and name it after your project. When you open it again you can carry on where you left off, and changes are saved as you make them. If you use iCloud, saved plans sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. A plan you haven’t saved is still there the next time you open Stitches. On a Mac, Stitches asks whether to save it when you close the window.

Mac

Stitches on the Mac

You buy Stitches once for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  • Pointer and keyboard

    Drag a decrease along the diagram, or click it and use the arrow buttons. In a count field, the up and down arrow keys change the number.

  • Menus and shortcuts

    New Plan (⌘N), Save Plan (⌘S), Rename and Delete are in the File menu. Undo (⌘Z) says what it will undo, such as Undo Move Decrease.

  • Side by side

    Saved plans open next to the list. Step by Step opens in an inspector beside the plan.

Details

Options and accessibility

  • 01

    Increase and decrease types

    The decreases are k2tog, ssk and p2tog, and the increases are M1L, M1R, kfb, yo, M1P and pfb. A kfb is worked into one of your stitches and an M1 goes between two, and the spacing takes that into account. If you pick a purl increase or decrease, the rest of the row is purled too.

  • 02

    Edges and markers

    A flat row can have half a gap at each end. If the edge will show, as it does on an open front or a picked-up band, you can keep all the gaps the same size instead. In the round, the shaping mirrors around your marker when the numbers allow it.

  • 03

    Accessibility

    VoiceOver reads every gap and handle and tells you which are unbalanced. You can move or lock an increase or decrease with it, and it says when the counts won’t work. With Differentiate Without Color on, unbalanced spacing has stripes and diamonds as well as the orange. On iPhone and iPad, text follows your text size setting.

  • 04

    Offline, no account

    You don’t need a connection or an account, and there are no ads. The app follows your system’s light or dark setting unless you pick one in Settings.

Privacy

Stitches doesn’t collect your data

All Stitches has is the plans you make in it. It doesn’t track you or send anything to the developer.

On your devices

Saved plans are stored in the app on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and you can change or delete them at any time.

iCloud

If you use iCloud, Apple syncs your saved plans through your private iCloud storage. Stitches has no server of its own, and the developer can’t see your plans.

Stitches privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The developer does not collect your personal data through Stitches. Your plans are stored on your device and, when iCloud sync is available, in your private iCloud database. There is no app account, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information you enter

Stitches uses the stitch counts, shaping choices and plan names you enter to calculate placement and write knitting instructions. Saved plans remain in the app's storage on your device.

iCloud sync

Stitches uses Apple's SwiftData and CloudKit services to sync saved plans between devices signed in to your Apple Account. Those records are stored in your private iCloud database under Apple's control and your iCloud settings. The developer does not operate a server for Stitches or receive your plans. The calculator can be used offline; sync resumes when Apple can connect your devices to iCloud.

What the developer collects

The developer collects no data. Stitches has no analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, crash reporting service or third-party SDKs. The developer cannot see the contents of your plans or how you use the app.

Your control

You can edit and delete saved plans in Stitches. You can manage Stitches' iCloud data and iCloud access in the Apple Account settings on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Deleting the app from an iPhone or iPad removes its local data. On a Mac, deleting the app can leave its local data in your user Library folder. In either case, iCloud records or device backups may remain under your Apple settings until you remove them there.
