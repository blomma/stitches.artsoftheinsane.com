How it works

Even spacing you can adjust

Stitches starts by spacing everything evenly. If that doesn’t suit your stitch pattern, you can move things around.

01 / Setup

Enter your numbers

Enter your stitch count and how many stitches to increase or decrease. Choose flat or in the round and the increase or decrease you’re using, and for a flat row, how much to leave at each edge. Stitches spaces them evenly, shows where each one falls on a diagram and gives you the stitch count after the row.

02 / Adjusting

Move the shaping

Even spacing can put a decrease in the middle of a cable, or you might want it lined up with the lace below. Touch and hold a decrease to slide it along (on a Mac, drag it), or tap it and move it one stitch at a time. The gaps on either side change to match. Lock any that need to stay where they are, then rebalance to space the rest evenly around them. Orange marks spacing that no longer mirrors the other side. The written row updates as you go, and you can undo any change. To check where the gaps of 7 and 8 ended up, tap a gap size under the diagram and each gap of that size gets a dot, in a different colour for each size.

03 / The written row

Written out like a pattern

Stitches writes the row the way a pattern would, for example “Decrease row (RS): K5, k2tog, [k10, k2tog] 4 times, k5 (55 sts).” Repeats can use brackets, parentheses or asterisks. For a long row, Step by Step breaks it into separate steps. You can share the row to Notes, Messages or another app.

04 / Saved plans

Save plans for later

You can save a plan under your project’s name. A saved plan opens in the planner (on a Mac, beside the list), and any changes you make to it are kept. With iCloud, saved plans sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. A plan you haven’t saved is still there the next time you open Stitches, and on a Mac, Stitches asks whether to save it before the window closes.

Mac

Stitches runs on the Mac too

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac, and saved plans sync between them over iCloud.

  • Pointer and keyboard

    Drag a decrease along the diagram, or click it and step it with the arrow buttons. The arrow keys change your stitch counts.

  • Menus and shortcuts

    New Plan, Save Plan, Rename and Delete are in the File menu, with ⌘N and ⌘S. The Undo menu item (⌘Z) says which change it will undo.

  • Side by side

    Saved plans open next to the list, and Step by Step opens in an inspector beside the plan.

Details

Options and accessibility

The options cover what most patterns ask for.

  • 01

    Increase and decrease types

    Decreases: k2tog, ssk or p2tog. Increases: M1L, M1R, kfb, yo, M1P or pfb. Stitches accounts for a kfb being worked into an existing stitch and an M1 not, so the counts are correct. If you pick a purl increase or decrease, the rest of the row is purled too.

  • 02

    Edges and markers

    On a flat row, you can leave half a gap at each end to centre the shaping, or keep all the gaps the same size when the edge will be visible, such as on an open front or a picked-up band. In the round, the shaping is mirrored around your marker when the numbers allow it.

  • 03

    Input and accessibility

    Drag a handle on the diagram, or tap or click it and nudge it with the arrow buttons. Select a gap to see its stitch count. VoiceOver reads every gap and handle, tells you when the spacing is unbalanced, and can move or lock any increase or decrease. It also tells you when the counts won’t work. With Differentiate Without Color turned on, uneven spacing is shown with stripes and diamonds as well as orange. On iPhone and iPad, text follows your text size setting.

  • 04

    Offline, no account

    Stitches works offline and doesn’t need an account. It can be light or dark, or follow your system setting. There are no ads or subscriptions.

Privacy

Stitches doesn’t collect your data

The only information Stitches has is the numbers and plan names you enter. It doesn’t track you or send anything to the developer, and there’s no account to create.

On your devices

The calculator works offline. Saved plans are stored in the app on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and you can change or delete them at any time.

iCloud

If you use iCloud, Apple syncs your saved plans through your private iCloud storage. There’s no Stitches server, and the developer can’t see your plans.

Privacy Policy — Stitches

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version: The developer does not collect your personal data through Stitches. Your plans are stored on your device and, when iCloud sync is available, in your private iCloud database. There is no app account, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Information you enter

Stitches uses the stitch counts, shaping choices and plan names you enter to calculate placement and write knitting instructions. Saved plans remain in the app's storage on your device.

iCloud sync

Stitches uses Apple's SwiftData and CloudKit services to sync saved plans between devices signed in to your Apple Account. Those records are stored in your private iCloud database under Apple's control and your iCloud settings. The developer does not operate a server for Stitches or receive your plans. The calculator can be used offline; sync resumes when Apple can connect your devices to iCloud.

What the developer collects

The developer collects no data. Stitches has no analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, crash reporting service or third-party SDKs. The developer cannot see the contents of your plans or how you use the app.

Your control

You can edit and delete saved plans in Stitches. You can manage Stitches' iCloud data and iCloud access in the Apple Account settings on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Deleting the app from an iPhone or iPad removes its local data. On a Mac, deleting the app can leave its local data in your user Library folder. In either case, iCloud records or device backups may remain under your Apple settings until you remove them there.
